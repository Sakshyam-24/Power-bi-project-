Nepal E-Commerce Data Dictionary
=================================

Dataset: Sales.csv
Records: 1,200 transactions
Date Range: January 2024 - December 2024

Column Definitions:
-------------------

OrderID (Integer)
    Unique identifier for each transaction
    Range: 1000 - 2199

Date (Date)
    Transaction date
    Format: YYYY-MM-DD
    Range: 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31

City (String)
    Customer location
    Values: Kathmandu, Pokhara, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Biratnagar, Chitwan

Category (String)
    Product category
    Values: Electronics, Clothing, Groceries, Home & Kitchen, Beauty, Books

Price (Decimal)
    Unit price in Nepalese Rupees (NPR)
    Range: 100 - 20,000

Quantity (Integer)
    Number of items ordered
    Range: 1 - 5

CustomerAge (Integer)
    Age of customer
    Range: 16 - 70

PaymentMethod (String)
    Payment type used
    Values: eSewa, Khalti, Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer

Rating (Decimal)
    Customer satisfaction rating
    Range: 1.0 - 5.0

TotalAmount (Decimal)
    Calculated: Price × Quantity
    Represents total order value in NPR

Month (String)
    Extracted from Date
    Values: January - December

Weekday (String)
    Extracted from Date
    Values: Monday - Sunday

AgeGroup (String)
    Customer age segment
    Values: 16-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-70

RevenueSegment (String)
    Order value tier
    Values: Budget (<2,000), Standard (2,000-5,000), Premium (5,000-10,000), Enterprise (>10,000)
